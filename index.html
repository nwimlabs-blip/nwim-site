NWIM Technology

The technology

Rather than automatically adding a new local electric drive, the architecture treats an existing air, vacuum, pressure or other energy flow as a potential source of useful mechanical work.

Mechanical architecture

The value is not tied to one housing or end device. The same underlying architecture is intended to be adapted to different mechanical tasks.

Existing energy flows

NWIM is designed around energy already present in the system, with the working module separated from a conventional local electric drive where the application permits.

Rapid development

Current prototypes are developed through a fast CAD → print → test → design-change cycle, enabling physical iteration before industrial engineering.

Industrial pathway

Industrial implementation requires dedicated work on materials, tolerances, bearings, balancing, tribology, service life and applicable certification.

Physically demonstrated

The underlying principle has been tested across several different device classes. These are proof-of-concept prototypes, not production products.

Potential application space

The demonstrated prototypes establish a starting point. Broader applications require separate technical, manufacturing and regulatory validation.

Consumer & light cleaning

Vacuum attachments, household appliances, garden tools and specialized accessories.

Professional & construction

Construction tools, surface treatment, grinding, cleaning and dust-intensive operations.

Heavy industry

Mining, oil & gas, industrial maintenance, robotic end-effectors and specialized pneumatic equipment.

Extreme / remote environments

Aviation maintenance, offshore and subsea systems, marine maintenance and underwater robotics.

Potential applications are shown as areas for investigation unless physically demonstrated. Product-specific performance and regulatory suitability require separate validation.
