01 / Platform

Not one tool.

The commercial proposition is therefore a transferable technology base that an industrial owner can adapt within its own engineering and manufacturing environment.

01 / SYSTEM EFFICIENCY

Do not create a second drive by default.

The philosophy is not anti-electric. A main motor may still create the pressure or vacuum. NWIM asks whether the existing flow can also perform useful mechanical work downstream.

02 / ARCHITECTURE

Move the source away from the working point.

Where the application allows it, the energy source can stay in a safer or more convenient location while the end module contains the mechanical conversion and output stages.

03 / ITERATION

CAD → print → test → redesign.

Current prototypes use rapid fabrication to explore the mechanism. The industrial step is disciplined engineering around defined loads, materials, tolerances and service life.

04 / OWNERSHIP

Developed for an industrial buyer.

NWIM is being positioned for direct acquisition by an industrial company that can select the highest-value applications and take the platform through product engineering and commercialization.

What still requires engineering

Materials and wear, manufacturing tolerances, bearing assemblies, clearances, balancing, lubrication, tribology, service life, real-world load testing and applicable certification.

Current prototypes are a basis for optimization and verification, not a substitute for industrial qualification.
